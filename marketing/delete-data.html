Delete Your Data

You have full control over your data. Here's what we store and how to delete it — with or without deleting your account.

What data we store

Data type What it includes Can delete without closing account?
Account info Email address, username Account deletion required
Game history Past games played vs AI Yes — via Settings
XP & progress XP total, rank, puzzle results Account deletion required
Purchase records Master's Vault entitlement Account deletion required

Delete specific data (keep your account)

  1. 1 Open the mindChess app
  2. 2 Tap the menu icon (☰) → Settings
  3. 3 Tap Clear Game History to erase your past games

Delete everything (close your account)

This permanently deletes your account, all game history, XP progress, and purchase records. This cannot be undone.

  1. 1 Open the mindChess app
  2. 2 Tap the menu icon (☰) → Settings
  3. 3 Tap Delete Account
  4. 4 Enter your password to confirm, then tap Delete My Account

All data will be permanently deleted within 30 days.

Need help or can't access the app?

Email us and we'll delete your data manually within 30 days. Please include the email address associated with your account.
