Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

mindChess ("we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use the mindChess Android application.

1. Information We Collect

Account information: When you create an account, we collect your email address. This is required to use the app.

Gameplay data: We store your game history, XP progress, puzzle results, and settings in Firestore (Firebase) linked to your account. This data is used to sync your progress and power in-app features.

Purchase information: If you purchase the Master's Vault, your purchase is processed through Google Play. We use RevenueCat to manage and verify your entitlements. We do not store or have access to your payment card information.

AI Coach interactions: When you use the AI Chess Coach, your questions are sent to our server to generate responses. We do not permanently store the content of these conversations.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and maintain your account
  • To save and sync your game progress, XP, and settings
  • To verify and unlock premium features you have purchased
  • To provide AI coaching responses
  • To improve the app

3. Third-Party Services

mindChess uses the following third-party services:

  • Firebase (Google): Authentication and Firestore database. Firebase Privacy Policy
  • RevenueCat: In-app purchase management and entitlement verification. RevenueCat Privacy Policy
  • Google Play Billing: Payment processing for the Master's Vault purchase. Governed by Google's privacy policy.

4. Data Retention

We retain your account data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account through the app's Settings page, we will delete your account information, gameplay data, and purchase records from our systems within 30 days.

5. Data Security

Your data is transmitted over HTTPS and stored in Firebase, which provides industry-standard security. We do not sell your data to third parties.

6. Children's Privacy

mindChess is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

7. Your Rights

You may:

  • Access your data by viewing your profile within the app
  • Delete your account and all associated data via Settings → Delete Account
  • Contact us to request a copy of your data

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by updating the date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

9. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at: support@mindchess.study
