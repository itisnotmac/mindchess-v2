Delete Your Account

You can delete your mindChess account and all associated data directly from within the app.

⚠️ This action is permanent. Deleting your account will erase your game history, XP progress, and purchase records. This cannot be undone.

How to delete your account in the app

  1. 1 Open the mindChess app on your Android device
  2. 2 Tap the menu icon (☰) in the top corner to open the navigation menu
  3. 3 Tap Settings
  4. 4 Scroll down and tap Delete Account
  5. 5 Enter your password to confirm, then tap Delete My Account

Your account and all associated data — including your profile, game history, and purchase records — will be permanently deleted within 30 days of your request.

Can't access the app?

If you no longer have access to the app and need your account deleted, email us and we'll handle it manually within 30 days.
